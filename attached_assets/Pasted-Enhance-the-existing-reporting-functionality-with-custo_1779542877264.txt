Enhance the existing reporting functionality with custom date ranges 
and marketing-specific report types.

=== REPORT BUILDER ===

Route: /reports (enhance existing reports page if it exists, or create)

REPORT TYPES (tabs):
  Tasks Report | Content Report | Company Report | HubSpot Report | Credits Report

--- TASKS REPORT ---
Filters:
  Date range: custom date picker (from/to) OR presets 
    (Today | This Week | This Month | Last Month | This Quarter | Custom)
  Company: multi-select dropdown (all or specific)
  Assigned to: multi-select user dropdown
  Category: multi-select from task_categories
  Status: multi-select
  Pillar: multi-select from content_pillars

Output metrics:
  - Total tasks in range
  - Completed on time vs completed late vs not completed
  - Average completion time (created_at to completed_at)
  - By person: completion rate, tasks assigned vs completed
  - By company: task volume, completion rate
  - By category: volume breakdown
  - Credit consumption (sum of credit_cost on completed tasks)
  - Overdue rate %

Chart types:
  - Bar chart: tasks by status over time
  - Pie: tasks by category
  - Line: task volume by week over date range

Export: CSV + PDF button

--- CONTENT REPORT ---
Filters: same date range options + company + platform + pillar + status

Output metrics:
  - Content items created in range
  - Published vs draft vs scheduled
  - By platform breakdown (pie chart)
  - By pillar breakdown
  - Posts per day/week average
  - GBP specific: posts by type (What's New, Event, Offer, Product)

--- COMPANY REPORT ---
Per-company scorecard for selected date range:
  Table: Company | Tasks Created | Completed | Overdue | Content Published | 
    HubSpot Status | Credits Used | Onboarding %

--- HUBSPOT REPORT (if connected) ---
Pulls from HubSpot API for selected date range:
  Contacts created | Deals created | Deals closed | 
  Email campaigns sent | Average open rate | Average click rate |
  Social posts published | Top performing post

--- SAVE REPORT PRESETS ---
"Save This Report" → saves filter configuration with a name
"My Saved Reports" → list of saved presets, click to re-run instantly
Scheduled Reports: set a report to auto-email to specified addresses 
  on a weekly/monthly cadence
