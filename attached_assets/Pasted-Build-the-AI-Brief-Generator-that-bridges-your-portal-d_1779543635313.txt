Build the AI Brief Generator that bridges your portal data with Galaxy AI 
(and other AI tools) for content creation.

Route: /company/:companyId/ai-brief  (also accessible from content calendar)

=== TWO-PANEL LAYOUT ===

LEFT PANEL — Brief Builder (inputs):

Step 1 — Content Goal (radio buttons with icons):
  📍 Google Business Profile Post
  📱 Social Media Image  
  🎥 Social Media Video
  📧 Email Campaign Banner
  📝 Blog Feature Image
  📣 Ad Creative
  📰 Newsletter Header
  🎤 Podcast Thumbnail
  💼 Case Study Visual

Step 2 — Platform (auto-selected based on goal, override allowed)

Step 3 — Content Pillar (dropdown — loads this company's active pillars)
  Shows pillar description + tone notes as helper text below

Step 4 — Month / Campaign (text input)
  "June 2025 promotions" or "Summer home improvement season"

Step 5 — Specific Topic or Subject (textarea)
  "Completed 4,200 sq ft luxury kitchen remodel in Naples FL for the Johnson family"

Step 6 — Visual Style (dropdown):
  Photorealistic | Illustrated | Bold/Graphic | Minimalist | 
  Before & After Split | Behind the Scenes | Professional Headshot Style |
  Luxury/High-End | Construction Site | Residential Exterior

Step 7 — Include in Output (checkboxes):
  [x] Image generation prompt (for Galaxy AI)
  [x] Caption / body copy prompt
  [x] Hashtag suggestions
  [x] Call-to-action options (3 variants)
  [x] Google Business post (ready to paste)
  [ ] Email subject line variants (3 options)
  [ ] LinkedIn article outline

"Generate Brief" button

RIGHT PANEL — Generated Output:

Prompt assembly on server side:
  Load matching template from ai_prompt_templates WHERE content_goal = X
  Substitute variables from:
    brand_profiles (brand voice, colors, target audience, UVP, geographic focus)
    content_pillars (pillar name, description, tone_notes)
    form inputs (topic, seasonal context, visual style)
    companies (name, industry)
  Return assembled prompts

Display assembled prompts in labeled, copy-able sections:

  🖼️ IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT
  Subheading: "Paste directly into Galaxy AI"
  [Copy] button | full prompt text in a code-style box
  Character count shown (Galaxy AI context limit aware)

  ✍️ CAPTION BRIEF
  [Copy] | prompt text

  #️⃣ HASHTAGS
  [Copy All] | individual hashtag chips with individual copy

  📣 CALL TO ACTION OPTIONS
  3 CTA variants each with [Copy]

  📍 GOOGLE BUSINESS POST (ready to paste)
  [Copy] | formatted post within 1,500 char limit | char counter shown

  📧 EMAIL SUBJECT LINES (if selected)
  3 subject line variants with [Copy]

ACTION BUTTONS:
  "Save as Draft Calendar Item" → 
    Creates content_calendar_items record with:
      company_id, pillar_id, platform, body_copy = generated caption,
      ai_prompt_used = the image prompt, status = 'draft',
      scheduled_date = today + 7 days (editable)
    Redirects to that calendar item in the content calendar

  "Copy Everything" → copies all generated content to clipboard as formatted text

=== TEMPLATE LIBRARY ===
Admin route: /admin/ai-templates
CRUD interface for ai_prompt_templates table.
Allows customizing the base prompt templates with {{variable}} substitution.
Each template previews with sample substitution.
